About the company

Multirajope Solutions Limited is a construction company headquartered in Kenya. We offer architectural and interior design, structural design, general contracting, renovation and repairs, road construction, construction project management and consultancy.

We are known for delivering on time, within budget and to a high standard, and for adopting new technology, investing in our people and weighing environmental and economic sustainability in every project.

A close focus on client satisfaction and a proactive approach to risk lets us tailor each solution to the client’s needs.

We aim not only to meet our clients’ needs but to exceed their expectations, with innovative solutions, meticulous project management and an unwavering commitment to quality. The construction industry is changing, and MSL is leading the charge.C.P.M Makau King’oku, Company CEO

Our vision

To be a leading construction company in Kenya and the broader East African region, recognised for innovation, exceptional service and an unswerving commitment to quality. We aim to redefine the industry by integrating sustainable practices, advanced technologies and a people-centred approach, creating lasting impact on communities, environments and economies.
